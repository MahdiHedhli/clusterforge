# SEC-300 practical: The customer security review

**Status:** Draft assessment; runtime not verified. **Suggested duration:** four 30-45 minute blocks, with safe checkpoints.

## Prerequisites

SEC-200 and K8S-200 evidence, the relevant K8S-300 control qualifications, and the SEC-300 lessons. Use the [common rubric](README.md), [customer brief](../curriculum/practicals/levels-200-300/customer.json) and [incident packet](../curriculum/practicals/levels-200-300/incident.json).

## Assignment

You are reviewing a proposed AI training and serving environment for a fictional customer. Obtain missing requirements, propose a defensible design, identify its most important attack paths, and respond to changing information. Do not assume proprietary platform implementations or assurances.

## Block 1: discovery and architecture

Ask targeted discovery questions. Build a requirements register and annotated identity/data/control-plane diagram. Compare at least two isolation designs using the same evaluation criteria. Identify the highest-impact unknowns and their owners.

## Block 2: threat model and control proof

Present three plausible attack paths and the controls that break them. Demonstrate one approved live control with a positive and a negative test, using the namespace lab or validated prior evidence. State exactly which architecture claim it supports and which hardware/platform claims it cannot prove. Include model/checkpoint integrity and recovery in the design.

## Block 3: incident and requirement inject

Investigate the synthetic incident packet. Separate facts, inferences and unknowns, propose proportionate containment and explain evidence preservation. The instructor changes one operational requirement and one evidence assumption. Update the recommendation rather than defending an outdated hypothesis.

## Block 4: customer defense

Deliver a five-minute technical briefing and a one-minute executive summary. Hand over a requirement/control/evidence matrix, prioritized findings, one engineering-ready gap, an exception with an expiry/removal test, and the next verification action for each unresolved high-impact claim.

## Passing evidence

Apply the common rubric. Safety and honest representation are mandatory. Unsupported claims of operator exclusion, complete tenant isolation, legal compliance or proven exfiltration fail the relevant reasoning gate until corrected.

An analytical attempt may proceed without a live environment, but must be labeled ANALYTICAL_ONLY. Full practical sign-off requires the live positive/negative control proof and its prerequisites. No public commit count, agent-written document or simulated output can substitute for learner evidence.

## Cleanup and progression

Restore any live assessment changes through their approved owners and preserve safe resume state. Record remaining SEC-300 gaps explicitly. Advanced hardware/firmware validation and confidential-compute engineering become SEC-400/500+ work rather than silently expanding this assessment.
