# ClusterForge 200/300 learning route

**Release:** Curriculum v0.2. **Status:** authored Draft material; runtime and learner qualification remain separate.

This route connects four tracks without requiring four disconnected courses. Work in 20-45 minute gaps when appropriate, preserve safe checkpoints, and let required meetings/training take priority. Progress follows demonstrated competencies, not a deadline or a count of public commits.

## Entry and environment

Use existing K8S-100/SEC-100 evidence or demonstrate the entry skills. Do not require a learner to repeat completed work because a public progress file is absent. Start with a healthy, explicitly identified lab. Infrastructure preparation is separate from a deliberately injected incident.

Read the [learning contract](../docs/learning-contract.md), [practical environment contract](practicals/levels-200-300/README.md), [assessment rubric](../assessments/README.md) and [session record](../templates/candidate-zero-session.md).

## Recommended order

1. K8S-200-01 through 03 alongside SEC-200-01/02: reliable operations, identity and permission testing.
2. K8S-200-04 through 06 alongside SEC-200-03 through 06: storage/lifecycle reasoning and operational controls.
3. K8S-300-01 through 04: attack paths, admission, network enforcement, secrets and artifacts.
4. Begin SEC-300-01 through 04 using those observations. Add K8S-300-05/06 when approved audit/runtime prerequisites are available.
5. Complete SEC-300-05/06 and the four track gates, recording unavailable live components as open qualifications rather than simulated passes.

The first concrete 200-level session is [K8S-200-01](kubernetes/k8s-200/01-rollouts.md), unless a current evidence record shows it is already satisfied.

| K8S-300 control tests -> SEC-300 architecture | Link each observation to a requirement and state proof limits |
| K8S-300 incident timeline -> SEC-300 leadership | Add proportional containment, owners, operational tradeoffs and communication |

Attach evidence identifiers to each claim. Do not copy a completion checkbox between tracks.

## Qualification states

Use separate fields for content maturity, static validation, runtime validation and learner progress. Draft content may be useful for guided practice while still requiring qualification. NOT_RECORDED means unknown learner status, not failure or inactivity. ANALYTICAL_ONLY means the written reasoning was assessed without claiming a live-control demonstration.

This release supplies instructor fixture contracts and synthetic case packets, not runnable manifests or a universal runner. In lesson prose, a supplied fixture means one supplied and qualified by the instructor before the live exercise. Do not assume local or uncommitted experiments exist upstream. Advanced integrations are explicit prerequisites and are not reported as installed or tested by this release.
